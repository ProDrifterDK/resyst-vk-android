Spanish Hunspell dictionary (es_CL.dic / es_CL.aff in this directory)
=====================================================================

Source: the RLA-ES Spanish dictionary by Santiago Bosio, as packaged by Debian in
hunspell-es 1:24.2.1-1 (source package libreoffice-dictionaries 1:24.2.1-1). On Debian,
/usr/share/hunspell/es_CL.{dic,aff} are symlinks to es_ES.{dic,aff}; the files here are byte
copies of those (see SHA256SUMS).
