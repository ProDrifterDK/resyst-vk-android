Resyst VK - emoji data (assets/emoji/)
======================================

emoji.txt
    The emoji panel's catalog: every fully-qualified emoji of Unicode Emoji 18.0, in the Unicode groups and order, with
    its skin-tone variants. Generated by scripts/gen_emoji.py from emoji-test.txt (Unicode Emoji 18.0,
    https://www.unicode.org/Public/emoji/18.0/emoji-test.txt, sha256 8f3735cda1f92a779d78af67cf86066bb1f07143dc22f2ac29394d9bc57ab21a).

search.txt
    The emoji search index (names and keywords in Spanish and English). Generated by scripts/gen_emoji_search.py from
    the Unicode Common Locale Data Repository (CLDR), release 49 (https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr, tag release-49):
    common/annotations/{es,es_419,en}.xml and common/annotationsDerived/{es,es_419,en}.xml, pinned by sha256 in the
    generator. Changes: the names and keywords of the catalog's emoji only, folded to lower case without accents and
    split into words for lookup, plus a few keywords of Resyst's own (scripts/data/emoji-search-extra.tsv, each with its
    reason; GPL-3.0-or-later). The CLDR names are kept as CLDR writes them.
