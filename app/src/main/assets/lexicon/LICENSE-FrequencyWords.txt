Resyst VK's es.txt and en.txt in this directory are adapted from the FrequencyWords Spanish and
English lists (the most frequent 25 000 / 20 000 entries, lowercased and filtered to letter words
with inner apostrophes; scripts/gen_lexicon_assets.py). They are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0,
the license of the lists they come from. Attribution: Hermit Dave, FrequencyWords. Changes: cut to
the top entries and filtered as described above; the Spanish list is also re-ranked at runtime by
the rules in regional/ (Resyst VK's own).
