Resyst VK - context-offer model (assets/context/wt-m2.vkng)
==========================================================

A word-trigram model of Spanish used on the phone, offline, to offer a likely alternative for a typed word in the
suggestion bar ("Sugerencias por contexto"). It holds counts of word sequences, no text.

Built from:

  * Wikipedia contributors, "Wikipedia, la enciclopedia libre", https://es.wikipedia.org
    Spanish Wikipedia database dump of 2026-11-01, part 1 (eswiki-20261101-pages-articles1.xml-p1p159400.bz2,
    https://dumps.wikimedia.org/eswiki/20261101/), plain article prose.
    Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0),
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

  * Tatoeba contributors, https://tatoeba.org
    Spanish sentences, per-language export of 2026-11-14 (spa_sentences.tsv.bz2).
    Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR),
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/ (37 of the sentences are CC0 1.0).

Changes: the texts were tokenized (lowercase words, numbers as one token), counted as word unigrams, bigrams and
trigrams (Tatoeba weighted x8), pruned by count and quantised; no text is kept. Provenance (inputs, sha256s, builder
commit, settings): wt-m2.provenance.json; builder: tools/ngram-build/ in the source repository.
